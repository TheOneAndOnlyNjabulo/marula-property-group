# Escalation Policy

## 1. When to escalate

Escalate a ticket immediately if: an Emergency response target is at risk of being missed, a tenant explicitly
requests escalation after an SLA has already lapsed, the assigned vendor is unreachable or unable to attend, or
the issue turns out to be more severe than originally classified once a technician is on-site.

## 2. Escalation path

Helpdesk agent → duty Facilities Manager → Regional Operations Manager → executive on-call. Each level has 30
minutes to acknowledge an Emergency escalation and 4 business hours to acknowledge an Urgent or Routine
escalation before it automatically moves to the next level.

## 3. After-hours escalation

Outside business hours, Emergency escalations go directly to the duty Facilities Manager's after-hours line,
bypassing the helpdesk agent tier. See the After-Hours & Emergency Callout Policy for the on-call roster and
authorization limits.

## 4. Tenant-initiated escalation

A tenant may request escalation at any time by replying to their ticket reference or contacting the building
office directly. A tenant-initiated escalation must be acknowledged within 1 business hour regardless of the
ticket's original priority.

## 5. Documentation requirements

Every escalation must be logged against the original ticket with a timestamp, the reason for escalation, and the
name of the person or role it was escalated to. Escalations are reviewed monthly as part of vendor and helpdesk
performance reporting.

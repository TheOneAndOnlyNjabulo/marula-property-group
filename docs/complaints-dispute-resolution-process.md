# Complaints & Dispute Resolution Process

## 1. Scope

This process covers service complaints that are not tied to a single open maintenance ticket — for example,
dissatisfaction with how a past ticket was handled, or a complaint about staff or vendor conduct. Disputes over a
specific repair's cost allocation are handled under the Lease Damage vs. Landlord Responsibility Policy instead.

## 2. Submission channels

Complaints may be submitted through the tenant app, in writing to the building office, or by email to the
regional operations mailbox.

## 3. Resolution stages

Stage 1: frontline resolution by the building office within 3 business days. Stage 2, if unresolved: management
review by the Regional Operations Manager within 10 business days. Stage 3, if still unresolved: a formal written
response from Marula's executive team within 20 business days.

## 4. Timeline commitments

Each stage's timeline is measured from the point the complaint is formally logged, not from the underlying
incident date. A tenant is notified in writing whenever a complaint moves to the next stage.

## 5. Escalation to external bodies

For residential leases, a tenant who remains unsatisfied after Stage 3 may refer the matter to the Rental Housing
Tribunal in their province. This right exists regardless of Marula's internal process and must not be withheld or
delayed as a condition of internal resolution.

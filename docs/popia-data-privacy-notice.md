# POPIA Data Privacy Notice

## 1. Purpose and scope

This notice explains how Marula Property Group collects, uses, and protects personal information in connection
with the maintenance helpdesk, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 2. Personal information collected

The helpdesk collects tenant contact details (name, unit number, phone number, email), the content of maintenance
tickets and any photos submitted with them, and building access logs where relevant to a ticket (for example, a
security or access-control complaint).

## 3. Lawful basis and purpose of processing

Personal information is processed only for the purpose of logging, triaging, and resolving maintenance requests,
communicating with tenants about those requests, and internal performance reporting on response times. It is not
used for marketing without separate, explicit consent.

## 4. Data sharing with vendors and contractors

Only the minimum information a vendor needs to attend a ticket (unit number, access instructions, issue
description) is shared with the dispatched vendor. Vendors are contractually bound to use that information only
for the purpose of completing the assigned work, per their onboarding agreement described in Vendor/Contractor
Onboarding & Compliance.

## 5. Retention period

Ticket records, including any photos, are retained for 5 years after closure to support warranty claims, dispute
resolution, and insurance purposes, after which they are securely deleted.

## 6. Tenant rights

A tenant may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information held in connection with
the helpdesk, subject to Marula's lawful retention obligations. Requests should be directed to Marula's
Information Officer via the building office.

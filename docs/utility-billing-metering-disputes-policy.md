# Utility Billing & Metering Disputes Policy

## 1. Sub-metering overview

Water and electricity are sub-metered per unit and billed monthly based on the meter reading for that unit.

## 2. Dispute submission process

A tenant disputing a utility bill must submit the dispute in writing with the specific reading or amount in
question within 30 days of the bill date. Disputes submitted after 30 days are reviewed but do not guarantee a
retroactive adjustment.

## 3. Meter fault versus billing error distinction

If the meter itself appears faulty (no movement over a full billing cycle despite normal usage, or an implausible
jump in reading), this is routed as a Plumbing or Electrical maintenance ticket per the appropriate trade
category, classified Routine unless the fault also affects supply. If the meter is functioning correctly but the
bill was calculated incorrectly, it is routed to accounts as a billing error, not a maintenance ticket, and
carries no maintenance SLA.

## 4. Resolution timeline for disputes

Billing disputes are resolved within 10 business days of submission, including any necessary re-read or
recalculation.

## 5. Estimated billing during meter fault periods

While a faulty meter is under repair, billing reverts to the unit's trailing 3-month average consumption rather
than an arbitrary estimate, and is trued up against the actual reading once the meter is repaired.

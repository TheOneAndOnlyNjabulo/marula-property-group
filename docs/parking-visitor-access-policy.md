# Parking & Visitor Access Policy

## 1. Allocated parking and visitor bay rules

Each tenant is allocated a fixed number of parking bays per their lease. Visitor bays are shared and time-limited;
they are not to be used for tenant staff overflow parking.

## 2. Access control faults

A malfunctioning main entry boom gate or the primary pedestrian access-controlled door is Urgent, since it leaves
a property either unsecured (stuck open) or fully inaccessible (stuck closed). A single faulty access card reader
with an alternative entry point available is Routine.

## 3. Visitor registration procedure

Visitors must be pre-registered by the host tenant through the building app or with the building office at least
30 minutes before arrival for access to be granted at the boom or door.

## 4. Lost access card or remote replacement

A lost access card or remote is deactivated immediately on report and a replacement is issued within 2 business
days, billed to the tenant at the standard replacement fee — this is not a maintenance ticket and does not carry
an SLA response/resolution target.

## 5. Security incident versus maintenance ticket distinction

An access control equipment fault (the gate itself is broken) is a maintenance ticket under this policy. A
suspected unauthorized entry or security breach is a security incident, not a maintenance ticket, and must be
routed to building security and, where appropriate, the South African Police Service — not triaged through the
maintenance helpdesk.

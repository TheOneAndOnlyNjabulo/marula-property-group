# After-Hours & Emergency Callout Policy

## 1. Definition of after-hours

After-hours means weekdays 17:00–07:00, all day Saturday, Sunday, and South African public holidays.

## 2. What qualifies for an after-hours callout

Only Emergency-classified tickets, plus Urgent security or access-control failures that leave a property
unsecured, qualify for an after-hours vendor callout. All other Urgent and Routine tickets are logged and
scheduled for the next business day.

## 3. Callout response times

An after-hours Emergency callout must be dispatched within 2 hours of logging (versus the 1-hour daytime target),
reflecting realistic vendor mobilisation time outside business hours. Elevator entrapments remain a sub-30-minute
response regardless of the hour.

## 4. Vendor callout authorization and cost approval

The duty Facilities Manager may authorize after-hours callout costs up to R5,000 without further sign-off.
Anything above that threshold requires verbal authorization from the Regional Operations Manager, to be confirmed
in writing within 24 hours.

## 5. Tenant safety instructions pending arrival

While waiting for an after-hours technician, tenants should be advised: shut off the water mains for active
flooding, switch off the relevant circuit breaker (not attempt any repair) for electrical faults, and evacuate
immediately and call emergency services first for any suspected gas leak or fire — a maintenance ticket is never a
substitute for calling emergency services when life is at risk.

# Common Area Maintenance Policy

## 1. Scope

Lobbies, corridors, stairwells, parking areas, gardens and grounds, and shared ablution facilities.

## 2. Reporting responsibility

Any tenant, visitor, or staff member may log a common area ticket — it is not restricted to the tenant whose unit
is nearest the issue, since common areas are shared property.

## 3. Priority guidance

A safety hazard in a common area — a broken stair railing, a large trip hazard, inadequate lighting on an
emergency route — is Urgent at minimum, and Emergency if it presents an immediate fall or injury risk. A cosmetic
issue (scuffed paint, worn carpet) is Routine.

## 4. Cost allocation

Common area repairs are funded from the building's shared operating budget and levied across all tenants per the
applicable lease or body corporate terms — they are never billed to a single tenant, even if that tenant reported
the issue.

## 5. Scheduled cleaning and grounds maintenance

Common areas receive scheduled cleaning and grounds maintenance on a fixed weekly calendar; these visits are
logged as Routine maintenance, not opened as reactive tickets.

# HVAC Maintenance Policy

## 1. Scope

Covers split-unit air conditioners, central air handling units, and mechanical ventilation systems in common
areas and tenanted spaces.

## 2. Priority guidance

Loss of cooling to a server room, comms room, or any space with temperature-sensitive equipment is Emergency. Loss
of air conditioning in a fully tenanted office during a heat advisory (Level 2 or higher) is Urgent. A routine
service request, filter change, or minor performance complaint with no fault present is Routine.

## 3. Refrigerant handling compliance

Any work involving refrigerant must be performed by a technician certified under the applicable refrigerant
handling regulations and may never involve venting refrigerant to atmosphere. This is a compliance requirement,
not a preference — non-compliant vendors must be removed from the panel per Vendor/Contractor Onboarding &
Compliance.

## 4. Seasonal maintenance schedule

Central systems receive preventative servicing twice yearly, ahead of the summer cooling season (September) and
the winter heating season (April), to reduce Emergency and Urgent call volume during peak demand periods.

## 5. Filter and service reporting

Every service visit must log filter condition and any parts replaced, so recurring failures on the same unit can
be identified before they become Urgent or Emergency tickets.

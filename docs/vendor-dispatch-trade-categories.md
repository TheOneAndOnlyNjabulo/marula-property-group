# Vendor Dispatch & Trade Categories

## 1. Category list and definitions

Every ticket must be assigned exactly one of the following categories: Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Elevator/Lift,
Fire Safety Systems, Pest Control, Structural/General Building, Security/Access Control, Common Area/Grounds, or
Appliances.

## 2. Approved vendor panel per category

Each category has a panel of 2–4 pre-vetted vendors that have completed the onboarding process described in
Vendor/Contractor Onboarding & Compliance. Only panel vendors may be dispatched; a non-panel vendor requires
written sign-off from the Regional Operations Manager.

## 3. Dispatch rules

Dispatch the nearest available panel vendor for the ticket's category. For Emergency tickets, availability
overrides rotation — call every panel vendor in sequence until one confirms immediate attendance. For Urgent and
Routine tickets, dispatch rotates evenly across the panel to distribute volume and keep all vendors' response-time
data statistically meaningful for the performance review.

## 4. Multi-trade tickets

When a ticket genuinely spans two categories (for example, a water leak that has also damaged wiring), dispatch
the primary trade first. The primary trade's technician assesses on-site and requests a secondary dispatch only if
their own trade cannot resolve the issue alone — do not pre-dispatch both trades speculatively.

## 5. Vendor response confirmation requirement

A vendor is not considered "dispatched" until they confirm receipt and give an ETA. A ticket showing a vendor
notified but not confirmed within the applicable response window must be escalated per the Escalation Policy.

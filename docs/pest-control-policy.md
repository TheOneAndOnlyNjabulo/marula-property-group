# Pest Control Policy

## 1. Scope

Rodents, cockroaches, wood borer, and wasp or bee infestations in tenanted units and common areas.

## 2. Priority guidance

A bee or wasp swarm near an occupied entrance or high-traffic common area is Urgent, due to the risk of a sting
incident. An infestation posing a documented health risk (confirmed rodent activity in a food-preparation area,
for example) is Urgent. A general pest sighting with no immediate health or safety element is Routine.

## 3. Scheduled preventative treatment

Common areas and refuse points receive preventative treatment quarterly. Preventative visits are logged as
Routine maintenance, not as tickets against a specific complaint.

## 4. Tenant preparation requirements

Before an internal unit treatment, tenants must be given at least 24 hours' notice and clear instructions on
preparation (clearing cupboards, covering food preparation surfaces) — treatment may be rescheduled if a unit is
not adequately prepared, since re-treating without proper prep wastes the visit.

## 5. Chemical safety and re-entry periods

Every treatment must specify the product used and its re-entry period. The re-entry period must be communicated
to the tenant as part of the ticket close-out, not assumed to be common knowledge.

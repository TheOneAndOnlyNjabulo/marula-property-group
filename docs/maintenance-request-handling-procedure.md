# Maintenance Request Handling Procedure

## 1. Intake channels

Tickets may be submitted through the tenant app, the helpdesk phone line, email, or in person at the building
office. Whichever channel is used, the ticket must be captured in the same system so it is subject to the same
triage and SLA process.

## 2. Required information at intake

Every ticket must capture: the reporting tenant's unit/suite number and contact details, a description of the
issue, when it was first noticed, and whether it is currently active/ongoing (e.g. actively flooding versus
already stopped). Missing information should be requested immediately rather than guessed at during triage.

## 3. Triage steps

On intake, classify the ticket's category per Vendor Dispatch & Trade Categories and its priority per the Priority
Classification Policy, look up the applicable response and resolution targets in the SLA Response & Resolution
Times document, and draft the tenant-facing acknowledgement per the Tenant Communication Standards.

## 4. Assignment and dispatch

Once triaged, the ticket is assigned to a panel vendor per the dispatch rules and the vendor's confirmed ETA is
recorded against the ticket.

## 5. Close-out requirements

A ticket may only be closed once the tenant has confirmed the issue is resolved, the vendor has supplied
before/after photos where practical, and any invoice has been reconciled against the original scope of work. A
ticket closed without tenant confirmation must be reopened if the tenant subsequently reports the issue persists.

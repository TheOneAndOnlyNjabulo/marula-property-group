# General FAQ

## 1. How do I submit a maintenance ticket?

Through the tenant app, by phone to the helpdesk line, by email, or in person at the building office, per the
Maintenance Request Handling Procedure. Include your unit number, a description of the issue, and whether it is
currently active (for example, still flooding) so it can be triaged accurately.

## 2. How do I check my ticket status?

Ticket status is visible in the tenant app under your ticket reference number, and you will also receive proactive
updates on the cadence set out in the Tenant Communication Standards — hourly for Emergency tickets, daily for
Urgent, and at each status change for Routine.

## 3. What are the response times?

Response and resolution targets depend on the priority assigned to your ticket — see the SLA Response &
Resolution Times document for the full breakdown by tier.

## 4. Who pays for repairs?

Marula covers fair wear and tear and building system failures. Tenants are billed for damage caused by their own
negligence or misuse. See the Lease Damage vs. Landlord Responsibility Policy for worked examples.

## 5. How do I report an emergency after hours?

Call the helpdesk after-hours emergency line, which is answered 24/7 for Emergency-classified issues. See the
After-Hours & Emergency Callout Policy for what qualifies and what to do while you wait.

## 6. How is my personal information used?

Only for logging, triaging, and resolving your maintenance requests, and for related communication and reporting.
See the POPIA Data Privacy Notice for full detail and how to exercise your rights over your information.

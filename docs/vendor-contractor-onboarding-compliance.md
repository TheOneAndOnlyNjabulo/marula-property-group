# Vendor/Contractor Onboarding & Compliance

## 1. Onboarding requirements

Before joining any trade panel, a vendor must provide a valid company registration, a current tax clearance
certificate, and proof of public liability insurance with a minimum cover of R2,000,000.

## 2. Trade-specific certification requirements

Electricians must hold a valid registration eligible to issue a Certificate of Compliance under the Electrical
Installation Regulations. Plumbers performing geyser or gas-adjacent work must hold the relevant licensing.
Refrigerant-handling technicians must hold a current refrigerant handling certification. Lift technicians must
hold certification specific to lift maintenance under the applicable OHS Act regulations. A vendor missing the
certification required for a given ticket's category must not be dispatched to it, even if they are otherwise on
the panel for a related trade.

## 3. Site induction and safety requirements

Every vendor technician must complete a basic site safety induction before their first dispatch to a given
building, and must sign in with building security on each visit.

## 4. Performance review and panel removal criteria

Vendor performance is reviewed quarterly against SLA compliance rates logged per the SLA Response & Resolution
Times document. A vendor missing more than 20% of applicable response targets in a quarter, or found performing
work outside their certified scope, is removed from the panel pending review.

## 5. Payment terms

Approved invoices are paid within 30 days of submission, provided the invoice matches the original scope of work
recorded against the ticket.
